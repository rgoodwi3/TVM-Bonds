Calculator guide

Financial calculator or spreadsheet
NNumber of periods: years × payments per year.
I/YInterest rate per period, as a percent: enter 6 for 6%. With semiannual payments, use the annual rate ÷ 2.
PVA single amount today.
PMTAn equal payment at the end of every period (an ordinary annuity).
FVA single amount at the end.

Enter the four values you know, using 0 for any key the problem doesn't use, then solve for the fifth. In this lab, leave the fifth box blank and press Calculate. It works like the CPT key and rounds to the nearest dollar.

Signs

A financial calculator gives money paid out and money received opposite signs, so a positive PV produces a negative FV. In this lab signs don't matter: enter every amount as a positive number.

BA II Plus

  1. Clear old values: 2ND CLR TVM.
  2. Set one payment per period: 2ND P/Y, type 1, ENTER, then 2ND QUIT. Keep END mode (BGN off) for ordinary annuities.
  3. Type each value and press its key, for example 10 N and 6 I/Y.
  4. Press CPT, then the key you're solving for.

Excel or Google Sheets

=PV(rate, nper, pmt, fv) and =FV(rate, nper, pmt, pv), with the rate per period. For example, =PV(4%, 10, 3000, 50000). The result shows as negative; enter it without the sign.
